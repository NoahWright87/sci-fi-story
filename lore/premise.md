# Premise and broad chronology

**Status: Established (high-level); details remain open.**

1. An intelligent, ocean-dwelling alien civilization discovers Earth and initiates friendly first contact.
2. They offer humanity significant assistance, including supplies, food, and advanced technology, seeking exchange and cooperation.
3. They warn humans not to enter a particular region of space (the **Forbidden Zone**; working descriptive name).
4. Humans expand into space, with xenophobia and suspicion becoming important forces. Increased contact with alien security around the prohibited area feeds the belief that something is being hidden.
5. Humans secretly violate the boundary, ultimately amounting to an incursion/invasion.
6. This releases or activates an ancient combat-machine manufacturing threat.
7. Autonomous machines nearly exterminate both humanity and the aquatic aliens.
8. The long arc spans generations and includes difficult, incomplete human–alien integration and recovery.

**Nuance:** Humans are often the aggressors and broadly 'the bad guys' in the setup, but the story should preserve moral complexity rather than make every human identical.

**Open:** Exact eras, causes and mechanics of the machines' release, protagonists, major turning points, and final outcome.
