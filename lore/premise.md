# Premise and chronology

**Status:** Established high-level sequence; exact dates, names and story boundaries are open.

1. Intelligent aquatic aliens discover Earth and make peaceful first contact.
2. Their arrival helps unite Earth into a shaky global coalition. Humans and aliens sign a lucrative trade/technology agreement.
3. Humanity expands into space, including asteroid/resource mining. Over generations, xenophobia toward the aliens (derogatorily called “squids”) and distrust increase.
4. The aliens explicitly forbid human entry and mining in a region near their homeworld, warning of dangerous machines there. Human leadership interprets the warning as implausible mythology or superstition.
5. Earth's government secretly funds and launches a military incursion into that region, using suspicious or misleading security justifications and withholding the mission from the public.
6. Human interference reactivates a surviving ancient combat-machine factory. The alien homeworld raises a planetary shield and automated defenses; a small group of dissenting aliens attempts to help humanity.
7. The responsible human general flees toward Earth through a portal. The human/alien resistance fails to contain the machines.
8. Machines devastate Earth, nearly wiping out humanity. Some humans survive in offworld mining colonies. Machines continue patrolling space and hunting activity as though the war has never ended.
9. Eventually a joint human–alien force invades the factory on foot through its shadowed entrance. They discover it cannot be shut down, only directed.
10. A recurring hero, previously saved with alien cybernetic implants after a selfless injury, sacrifices himself by interfacing with the factory to become its permanent director.

The universe has **multiple major story arcs**, originally envisioned as different types of games. Do not assume a single protagonist, book or narrative format.
