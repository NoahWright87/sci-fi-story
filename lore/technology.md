# Technology and setting

**Status:** Established details with open engineering questions.

## Aquatic alien technology
- Advanced shielding, gravity manipulation, plasma and laser weaponry developed in response to the ancient machine threat.
- Multi-limbed aliens can control multiple complex systems simultaneously.
- Alien cybernetics can restore or augment a gravely injured human; a recurring hero later uses these implants to interface with the factory.

## Ancient machines
- Originally built as manually piloted war machines, then disastrously automated.
- Their faulty recognition treats other robots as friendly but organic life as hostile.
- Surviving automated manufacturing capacity is buried under a bombarded planet.
- With no director, the factory's output continues autonomous hostile operations.

## Locations
- Earth: initially unites into a fragile coalition, later nearly destroyed.
- Alien ocean homeworld: defended by a planetary shield and automated defense grid during the second machine war.
- Human offworld mining colonies: some survivors of Earth's destruction.
- Factory planet: extreme permanent day/night conditions, solar collectors and the shadowed Dark Place entrance.

## Open engineering questions
- Orbital geometry of the permanently shadowed location.
- Limits and costs of gravity manipulation, shields, portals and interstellar travel.
- Why orbital weapons cannot penetrate the remaining factory.
- How the factory survives, stores energy and maintains manufacturing after ages of dormancy.
