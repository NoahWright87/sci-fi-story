# Technology and design constraints

**Status: Established direction.**

- Aim for scientifically plausible technologies and consequences, except for explicitly accepted physics-defying elements such as limited faster-than-light travel and gravity manipulation.
- Alien ships hold water in place with manipulated gravity; the water is habitat and defense, but not invulnerable.
- Alien technology includes organic/biological systems, optical camouflage, neural interfaces/HUDs, and targeted anti-machine countermeasures.
- Alien weapons must be useful underwater, on land and in space.
- Humans adapt and combine organic and mechanical technology.
- Machine adversaries use inorganic armor/materials and comparatively direct autonomous sensing and combat behavior.
- Ship designs and environments should feel gritty, functional and alien, with a 'dirty sci-fi' sensibility.

**Open:** Limits/cost of FTL and gravity manipulation, energy budgets, weapon specifics, ship scales, interface limitations and technological diffusion.

_Source: author discussions, 2025-05-14, 2025-09-14, 2026-10-09; paraphrased._
