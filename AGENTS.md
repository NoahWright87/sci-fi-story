# Contributor and AI-agent instructions

This repository is a collaborative science-fiction world bible and writing workspace. The human author has final authority.

## Canon discipline
- **Established**: explicitly stated by the author. Do not quietly change or embellish.
- **Tentative**: an idea discussed but not approved as fact.
- **Open**: a question that still needs the author's answer.
- **Proposed**: original material from an AI or collaborator; never canon without explicit approval.
- Preserve uncertainty. Do not fill gaps with plausible-sounding inventions.
- Distinguish the author's ideas from extrapolations. Avoid treating a previous assistant's suggestion as author-approved.
- If sources conflict, record the conflict in `lore/open-questions.md` and ask before resolving.
- When importing notes from a conversation, retain a date/source note where known; paraphrase rather than pretend to quote.
- Do not rewrite or overwrite files in `human-input/` unless explicitly requested by the author.

## Working process
1. Read the README and relevant lore before drafting.
2. Put speculative material in `proposals/`, not in established lore.
3. Use small, reviewable PRs; summarize what changed and which assertions need approval.
4. Do not automatically merge PRs or silently promote proposals to canon.
5. Keep lore concise, linked, and internally consistent; prefer Markdown.
6. Treat `stories/` as narrative drafts, not automatic evidence of canon.
